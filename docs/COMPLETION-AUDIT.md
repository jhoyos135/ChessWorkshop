## Closed gaps from the earlier audit

The first guided game now reaches actual checkmate from the starting position and can reopen as a separate review game. Teaching imports now validate authored replies, phases, solved/assistance flags, demonstrations and full-game completion. Opponent moves/results have explicit accessible announcements, and rejected clicks explain the failure while preserving selection. Dedicated 3D special-move captures verify intermediate motion and the settled pieces. Clock suspension and in-game opponent-level changes now have dedicated behavioral coverage.

A subsequent import audit found contradictory lesson/game lifecycle fields and raw legacy completion states in library reads. Teaching now rejects injected game results, timed clocks, history selections and handoffs; normalized library copies preserve old exercise credit and expose the new guided-game step through Home and backups. Wrong legal lesson attempts now explain actual move consequences and legal replies, rather than only repeating the objective.

The same audit reproduced a session-JSON bypass of the standalone-FEN starting-turn check. JSON, PGN and FEN now use one check: the previous player's king cannot already be attacked, while a valid current player in check is allowed. Tests cover both colors and verify that rejected imports leave existing records unchanged.
